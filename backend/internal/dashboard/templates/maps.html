The map name is the one your game server sends with a match (GameplayBatch.map.map_id). Wall lines are a JSON file, {"lines":[[x0,y0,x1,y1],...]}, in centimetres in the coordinates of the match's positions, at most {{$d.MaxLines}} lines. A picture is a PNG of at most {{$d.MaxPNGMB}} MB and {{$d.MaxSide}} pixels on a side, with the world rectangle it covers; its top edge is max y. The gateway decodes a picture and writes it out again, so what it keeps and shows is its own copy of the pixels. An upload replaces the map's previous outline.

An outline is a drawing. With wall lines the replay also points out shots at a player whose line crosses a wall line. That is an aid for whoever watches the replay: it is not a finding, it has no score and nothing acts on it. Your game server can upload outlines too, with POST /v1/maps.
