A verdict is scored when its answer is known: at once on a calibration case, and on a real case when one of your reviewers confirms or dismisses what the players decided. Weight is (correct + 1) / (scored + 2), so a new reviewer starts at 0.50, and it is how much that reviewer's verdict counts in a case. A reviewer whose weight falls below {{fixed 2 $d.MinWeight}} with at least {{$d.MinScored}} scored verdicts is no longer handed cases.

Levels come from correct verdicts: {{range $i, $l := $d.Levels}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$l.Name}} at {{$l.Correct}}{{end}}. Reaching a level earns its badge. The gateway grants nothing in your game: your game server reads standing and new badges (GET /v1/review/standing, GET /v1/review/badges) and grants what you decide.
