A report is what one player said about another. It never bans, never puts anyone in the shadow pool and is not something the Oracle learns from: it tells a reviewer where to look. Reviewed and Dismiss record your conclusion and are in the audit log.{{if not $d.CanAct}} They need the reviewer role.{{end}} A dismissed report no longer counts toward the player's 30 days.

Weight is how the reporter's earlier reports turned out: (confirmed + 1) / (confirmed + dismissed + 2). A report is confirmed when the reported player has a standing ban or a session labelled a confirmed cheat; a new reporter starts at 0.50. The note is the reporter's own words and is deleted with the report after 90 days.
