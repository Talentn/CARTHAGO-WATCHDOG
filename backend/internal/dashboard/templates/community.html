Players see the match from above with the suspect named Suspect and everyone else numbered: no accounts, no findings, no scores, no report text. A case closes when {{$d.Set.Quorum}} players have given a verdict. It is decided when the answer with the most weight behind it has {{$d.AgreePct}}% of the weight, and undecided otherwise. A player's weight comes from how often their earlier verdicts were right.

A decided case is advice for you. It never bans, never ends a session, never puts anyone in the shadow pool and is not something the Oracle learns from. Confirm and Dismiss record your conclusion in the audit log and score the players who voted; a ban is still your own action on the player's page.{{if not $d.CanAct}} Confirm, Dismiss and calibration cases need the reviewer role.{{end}}{{if not $d.CanAdmin}} The settings need the admin role.{{end}}

A calibration case is a recorded match and a player whose answer you already know. It is handed to players among the real cases and scores them at once: a reviewer's first case is one, then about one in four, and every case of a reviewer who has been wrong more often than right.
