The player sees
Nothing. The handshake, the session token, the heartbeat answers and the alerts are those of a normal player; no alert is raised and the session is not ended. When the period is over the player is banned like anybody else, with a reference.
Your matchmaking
Asks the gateway who is in the pool, under your server key (POST /v1/shadow/status, GET /v1/shadow/pool), and matches those players with each other only. Without that the pool changes nothing for the other players.
Collected meanwhile
The sessions of a player in the pool are labelled "confirmed cheat" for the Oracle's training, the files they load count toward proposed known cheats, and money they trade or drop is marked dirty on the Economy page.
